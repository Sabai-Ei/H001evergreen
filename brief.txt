MANDATEDo not approach this project as simply "design a beautiful product page." The landing page must work as a coordinated product, conversion, search, content, and engineering system.

The goal is to create a high-performing product landing page that:
1. Makes H001 understandable within seconds.
2. Establishes WaterSource as a credible premium water-technology brand.
3. Communicates the differences between H001 and an ordinary water purifier.
4. Turns product technology into customer-understandable benefits.
5. Builds trust before asking visitors to buy or enquire.
6. Converts visitors into enquiries or purchases.
7. Performs strongly in Google Search.
8. Is structured clearly for Google AI Overviews and answer engines.
9. Is understandable and citation-friendly for ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and other generative search systems.
10. Works exceptionally well on mobile.
11. Feels premium, modern, and technologically advanced.
12. Loads quickly and performs well against Core Web Vitals.
13. Avoids exaggerated or unsupported health or medical claims.

10. LANDING PAGE INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
This should be a single conversion-focused landing page, not a multi-page sitemap.
01 - Header
Keep navigation extremely simple: Overview, Technology, Filtration, Features, Specifications, FAQ.
Primary CTA: Enquire Now or Order H001, depending on the confirmed sales flow.
Consider a sticky mobile CTA.
02 - Hero
Immediately communicate: H001 Alkaline Hydrogen-Rich Water Purifier.
Positioning direction: "Purify. Enrich. Heat. All from one intelligent system."
Use a large H001 product image, product name, strong value proposition, 3-4 key benefit indicators, pricing, promotional pricing if still valid, warranty information, primary CTA, and secondary CTA.
Quick feature row direction: 3-sec heating / RO purification / Hydrogen-rich water / UVC sterilization.
Do not fill the hero with technical paragraphs.
03 - One Machine. Multiple Functions.
Introduce the product mental model through three systems: Purify, Enrich, Heat.
Purify: advanced multi-stage filtration.
Enrich: hydrogen-rich water using dedicated hydrogen technology.
Heat: instant access to different temperatures.
04 - Why H001
Create a visually scannable benefit section.
Potential highlights: hydrogen-rich water, 3-second heating, multi-stage filtration, multiple temperature options, UVC sterilization, intelligent maintenance reminders.
Translate each item as Feature -&gt; Benefit -&gt; Evidence / Technology rather than copying PDF bullet points.
05 - Advanced Hydrogen Technology
Signature section explaining the process visually.
Suggested flow: Purified water -&gt; Electrolysis -&gt; Hydrogen -&gt; SuperMix -&gt; Hydrogen-rich water.
Use restrained motion such as animated water paths, subtle bubbles, or progressive diagram reveal.
Avoid gimmicky particle effects that reduce performance or readability.
06 - SuperMix Technology
Dedicated technical storytelling block.
Headline direction: "Engineered to Keep More Hydrogen in the Water."
Explain the gas-liquid mixing technology visually rather than using the highly technical source wording.
If the 3000-5000 ppb concentration claim is published, keep the unit visible, explain what it refers to, ensure WaterSource confirms it, and avoid presenting it as a medical outcome.
07 - Water Purification System
Make this one of the clearest technical sections.
Show Stage 1 PAC Composite Filter, Stage 2 200G RO Membrane, and Stage 3 Natural Trace Mineralized Carbon Filter.
Use a flow diagram: Tap Water -&gt; PAC -&gt; RO -&gt; Mineralized Carbon -&gt; Drinking Water.
Desktop may use an animated horizontal journey; mobile should become a clear vertical stepper.
08 - 3-Second Instant Heating
Make this highly visual.
Headline direction: "Hot Water Without the Wait."
Potential micro-interaction: Tap -&gt; 3 -&gt; 2 -&gt; 1 -&gt; Ready.
Keep animation fast and restrained.
09 - Water for Every Moment
Show why multiple temperature modes matter instead of presenting only a technical label.
Use cases can include room-temperature drinking water, warm water, milk preparation, coffee, tea, and hot water.
Make the appliance feel relevant to everyday life.
10 - Clean Water, Clean System
Focus on hygiene and maintainability.
Include UVC sterilization, removable / cleanable water tank, and water-system hygiene.
Build trust without using fear-based tap-water marketing.
11 - Smart Touch Control
Use an enlarged product-interface image.
Highlight status display, Normal, Warm, Milk, Coffee, Tea, Boiling, child lock, small cup, big cup, intelligent washing, filter reset, and status indicators.
UX message: advanced technology should still feel simple to use.
12 - Precise Water Dispensing
Explain quantitative dispensing using 150 ml, 300 ml, and 500 ml presets.
Show the quantities visually using different vessel sizes rather than another generic feature card.
13 - Smart Filter Maintenance
Headline direction: "H001 Tells You When It's Time."
Show the filter status visually.
Expected source intervals: PAC 6-12 months, RO 12-24 months, mineralized carbon 6-12 months.
Make maintenance feel simple rather than technical.
14 - Product Details
Use high-quality product renders: front, side, rear, detail, control panel, dispensing area.
Prefer subtle hover or scroll interactions over complicated 3D unless appropriate assets exist.
15 - Technical Specifications
Create a clean specification table.
Include verified fields such as model, rated water yield, water discharge, voltage/frequency, rated power, power consumption, water source, applicable water temperature, filter types, material, and product dimensions.
Do not publish dimensions until conflicting supplied measurements are confirmed.
16 - Offer / Purchase
Create a strong commercial section near the bottom.
Supplied quotation states usual price $3,388, promotion price $2,888, and 1-year on-site warranty; verify these before deployment.
CTA direction: Enquire About H001 or Get H001 Today.
Secondary CTA may be WhatsApp Us if WhatsApp is part of WaterSource's confirmed sales journey.
17 - FAQ
Use FAQ to support UX, SEO, AEO, and GEO.
Example intents: What is H001? What does it do? What is hydrogen-rich water? How is hydrogen-rich water produced? Does it filter tap water? What filtration system is used? How fast is hot water produced? What temperatures are available? Does it use UVC? How often are filters replaced? How is filter replacement indicated? Is installation required?
For questions not answered by the supplied product material, obtain confirmation rather than inventing an answer.
18 - Final CTA
End with a strong product shot.
Headline direction: "Upgrade the Way You Drink Water."
Supporting message: purification, hydrogen-rich water, and instant heating in one intelligent system.
Primary CTA: Enquire About H001. Secondary: Speak With WaterSource.

18. HEALTH &amp; CLAIMS RULE
Some supplied materials contain claims related to antioxidant activity, free radicals, absorption, and other health-related properties. Do not automatically turn those statements into stronger marketing claims.
Before publishing health-related copy:
Verify the claim
Confirm WaterSource approval
Confirm whether supporting evidence exists
Avoid implying disease prevention, treatment, or cure
Avoid transforming technical hydrogen characteristics into guaranteed health outcomes
Prefer product-focused statements about technology, purification process, hydrogen generation, temperature, convenience, hygiene features, and operation unless stronger claims are properly substantiated.
