工作原理：
How it works:
折叠纤维PP棉：创新采用折叠式设计，缩小了体积，过滤面积却大大增加，对进
入的自来水进行预处理，去除水中残留的泥沙、铁锈、沙石、悬浮物、杂物等大
颗粒杂质。
Folding fiber PP cotton: folding design, which reduces the volume, but greatly
increases the filter area. The tap water is pre-treated to remove the residual sediment,
rust, sand, suspended solids, debris and other large particles.
炭棒：使用比传统颗粒活性炭颗粒更小的炭棒滤芯，表面积更大，吸附能力更强，
过滤效果更好。进一步深层次的去除水中的余氯、异色、异味及部分有机物。
Carbon rod: The carbon filter element, which is smaller than traditional granular
activated carbon, has larger surface area, stronger adsorption ability and better filtration
effect. Further removal of residual chlorine, different color, peculiar smell and some
organic matter in the water.
注意事项：进水温度不能超过65℃，保证水的纯净。
Note: The inlet temperature should not exceed 65 ℃ to ensure the purity of the water.
