Why WaterSource hydrogen water / right water

Why the H001 focuses on right water, not just filtered water

Filtered water is only the first step. The H001 is designed to help remove everyday impurities first, then enrich the water with freshly generated WaterSource hydrogen and a smooth, weak-alkaline profile for a cleaner daily drinking experience.

That means every glass is positioned as more than ordinary purified water: fresh on demand, easy to drink, and made to support a modern wellness routine without relying on bottled WaterSource hydrogen water or separate devices.

The right-water sequence

  1. 01

    Purify: 3-stage, 4-layer filtration and 200G RO help remove sediment, rust, residual chlorine and dissolved impurities. Yet sufficient for high consumption requirements.

  2. 02

    Enrich: an independent electrolysis module creates WaterSource hydrogen, then SuperMix dissolves fine WaterSource hydrogen bubbles into the water. Enable stable provision of up to 3000 ppb-rich Hydrogen.

  3. 03

    Support: weak alkalinity, 18 minerals, negative potential and dissolved WaterSource hydrogen are positioned as gentle daily wellness support & maintain alkaline-acidic balance.

3-second instant heating

Hot water without the wait

Third-generation rare-earth thick-film heating warms water as it passes through an explosion-proof refined steel core, so heat reaches the water directly instead of slowly warming a stored tank.

Because water is filtered first and heated only when you ask for it, you avoid water that has been boiled and reboiled through the day. Thick-film heating also improves on the stability of older quartz-tube designs.

Smart filter maintenance

H001 tells you when it's time

You don't need to keep a calendar. The filter-life indicator stays dark in normal use, flashes orange when a filter reaches 80% of its service life, and stays lit once it is due. Filters are quick-connect, so replacement is a task you can do yourself.

  • Flashing orange — 80% of service life
