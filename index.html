Why Water Source hydrogen water / right water

Why the H001 focuses on right water, not just filtered water

Filtered water is only the first step. The H001 is designed to help remove everyday impurities first, then enrich the water with freshly generated Water Source hydrogen and a smooth, weak-alkaline profile for a cleaner daily drinking experience.

That means every glass is positioned as more than ordinary purified water: fresh on demand, easy to drink, and made to support a modern wellness routine without relying on bottled Water Source hydrogen water or separate devices.

The right-water sequence

  1. 01

    Purify: 3-stage, 4-layer filtration and 200G RO help remove sediment, rust, residual chlorine and dissolved impurities. Yet sufficient for high consumption requirements.

  2. 02

    Enrich: an independent electrolysis module creates Water Source hydrogen, then SuperMix dissolves fine Water Source hydrogen bubbles into the water. Enable stable provision of up to 3000 ppb-rich Hydrogen.

  3. 03

    Support: weak alkalinity, 18 minerals, negative potential and dissolved Water Source hydrogen are positioned as gentle daily wellness support & maintain alkaline-acidic balance.

3-second instant heating

Hot water without the wait

Third-generation rare-earth thick-film heating warms water as it passes through an explosion-proof refined steel core, so heat reaches the water directly instead of slowly warming a stored tank.

Because water is filtered first and heated only when you ask for it, you avoid water that has been boiled and reboiled through the day. Thick-film heating also improves on the stability of older quartz-tube designs.

H001 tells you when it's time
