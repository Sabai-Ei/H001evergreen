One machine. Three systems.

Purify / Enrich / Heat

Water is purified first, then enriched with hydrogen or heated on demand. Every glass is drawn from freshly filtered water rather than a repeatedly reheated tank.

01 / PURIFY

Three-grade, four-layer filtration

A PAC composite filter removes sediment, rust and residual chlorine. A 200G high-flux RO membrane follows. A natural trace mineralized carbon rod returns minerals such as strontium, potassium, calcium and magnesium.

TAP → PAC → 200G RO → MINERALIZED CARBON → DRINKING WATER

02 / ENRICH

Hydrogen dissolved by SuperMix

An independent electrolysis module generates hydrogen from purified water. SuperMix gas-liquid mixing shears it into micro- and sub-micron bubbles and dissolves it into the water under pressure.

ELECTROLYSIS → SUPERMIX → HYDROGEN-RICH WATER

03 / HEAT

Temperature in three seconds

Third-generation rare-earth thick-film heating brings water to temperature on demand, across six stages: room temperature, warm, milk, coffee, tea and boiling.

6 TEMPERATURE STAGES · 150 / 300 / 500 ML PRESETS
