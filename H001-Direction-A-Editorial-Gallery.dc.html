One machine.
Three systems.

H001 replaces a purifier, a hydrogen water generator and a hot-water dispenser. Water is filtered first, then enriched with hydrogen or heated on demand — so every glass is drawn from freshly purified water rather than repeatedly reheated storage.

01

Purify

A three-grade, four-layer filtration path: a PAC composite filter for sediment, rust and residual chlorine; a 200G high-flux RO membrane; and a natural trace mineralized carbon rod that returns minerals such as strontium, potassium, calcium and magnesium to the water.

PAC → 200G RO → Mineralized carbon

02

Enrich

An independent electrolysis module generates hydrogen from purified water. The SuperMix gas-liquid mixing system then shears that hydrogen into micro- and sub-micron bubbles and dissolves it into the water under pressure.

Electrolysis → SuperMix → Hydrogen-rich water

03

Heat

Third-generation rare-earth thick-film heating brings water to temperature in three seconds, with six temperature stages for room-temperature water, warm water, milk preparation, coffee, tea and boiling water.

3-second heating · 6 temperature stages
